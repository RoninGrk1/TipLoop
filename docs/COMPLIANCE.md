# Compliance notes

This repository includes policy pages for terms, privacy, refunds, fees, and crypto risk. They are starting templates, not legal advice.

Before enabling live payments:

1. Form a legal entity and obtain counsel review of the policy pages.
2. Determine whether you are a money transmitter / VASP in each launch market.
3. Complete NOWPayments (or successor) KYB.
4. Implement sanctions screening if required for your volume and geographies.
5. Publish a real privacy notice and appoint a contact email.
6. Confirm tax reporting obligations for creators and the platform.
7. Do not custody user funds without the matching license and controls.

Live payments remain disabled by environment flags until operators explicitly opt in.
